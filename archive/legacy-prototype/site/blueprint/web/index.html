Formal proof architecture

From the centered statistic to the exchangeable MGF bound

Seongchan Lee leads this formalization of joint work with his advisor and coauthor Ilmun Kim. The blueprint is indexed by functions and theorem roles. It records the exact boundary between closed Lean proofs and analytic statements that are still passed in explicitly.

Target
\[ \E\exp\left\{\lambda\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i(X_i-\overline X_N)\right\} \le \exp\left\{\frac{\lambda^2}{2}\Gamma_N \|\proj\widetilde w\|_2^2\right\}. \]
