A sharper Hoeffding bound for weighted sums of exchangeable random variables

Seongchan Lee · formalization lead
Joint paper with Ilmun Kim · advisor and coauthor

Led by Seongchan Lee, this project connects the joint paper's statistical argument to Lean declarations. Every stage is labeled as either a closed proof or a conditional reduction, so the machine-checked boundary is visible at a glance.

What Lean checks now

No source-layout knowledge is needed: declarations are grouped by mathematical role.

closed proof

centeredWeightedSum_eq_centered_coordinates

Proves that the projected dot product is exactly \(\sum_i w_i(X_i-\overline X_N)\), using checked zero-padding and finite-sum identities.

closed proof

bounded_centered_mgf

Instantiates the formal Hoeffding lemma with explicit measurability, centering, and interval hypotheses in the normalization used by the paper.

closed derivation

exchangeable_tail_bound_from_mgf

Derives the one-sided threshold from the MGF estimate with a checked sub-Gaussian Chernoff argument. The tail estimate is not assumed separately.

closed proof

projOnePerp_idempotent

Establishes the zero-sum projection invariant and shows that applying the projection twice changes nothing.

conditional reduction

exchangeable_mgf_bound

Reduces the centered theorem to the zero-sum MGF field of MainAnalyticInputs. The reduction is checked; the input structure is not yet constructed in the repository.
