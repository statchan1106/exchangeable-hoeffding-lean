Exchangeable Hoeffding in Lean 4

A blueprint for formalizing a sharper Hoeffding bound for weighted sums of bounded exchangeable random variables.

Project Thesis

The project demonstrates how Lean can make the proof architecture of a statistical concentration theorem explicit. The current development avoids global axioms by collecting the remaining hard mathematics in ProofObligations.

Finite Vectors and Centering

Definition: projection away from constants

ExchangeableHoeffding.projOnePerp

For \(z\in\mathbb{R}^N\), \[ P_{\mathbf{1}^{\perp}}z = z-\frac{1}{N}\left(\sum_i z_i\right)\mathbf{1}. \]

Lemma: centered statistic as projected dot product

ProofObligations.centeredWeightedSum_eq_dot_proj

\[ \sum_{i=1}^n w_i(x_i-\bar{x}_N) = \langle P_{\mathbf{1}^{\perp}}\tilde{w},x\rangle. \]

Main Bound

Theorem 2.1: exchangeable Hoeffding MGF bound

ExchangeableHoeffding.theorem_2_1

Assuming the proof-obligation interface, Lean derives \[ \mathbb{E}\exp\left( \lambda\sum_{i=1}^n w_i(X_i-\bar{X}_N) \right) \leq \exp\left( \frac{\lambda^2}{2}\Gamma_N \|P_{\mathbf{1}^{\perp}}\tilde{w}\|_2^2 \right). \]

Definition: finite-population inflation factor

ExchangeableHoeffding.Gamma

\[ \Gamma_N = \max_{1\leq s\leq\lfloor N/2\rfloor} \frac{N(N-s)}{s} \sum_{\ell=N-s}^{N-1}\frac{1}{\ell^2}. \]

Hamming Slice Reduction

Proposition 4.4: two-level extremizers

ProofObligations.proposition_4_4

On each sphere section \(\sum_i y_i=0\), \(\|y\|_2=\rho\), the maximum of the slice functional is attained by a vector with at most two distinct coordinate values.

Lemma 4.5: hypergeometric MGF bound

ProofObligations.lemma_4_5_hypergeom_mgf

The two-level reduction leaves a centered hypergeometric random variable, whose MGF is bounded by the factor \(B_{N,m}\).
