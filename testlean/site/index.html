A Sharper Hoeffding Bound for Weighted Sums of Exchangeable Random Variables

Seongchan Lee and Ilmun Kim

A Lean 4 companion project for the proof architecture of a sharper Hoeffding-type MGF bound for centered weighted sums of bounded exchangeable random variables. The Lean development is modularized under Testlean.Exchangeable_Hoeffding_ver2, with paper formulas, Lean declarations, and proof interfaces kept side by side.

Current verification boundary

The project uses no global axioms. Direct finite-algebra facts and endpoint computations are proved as Lean declarations, while deeper analytic ingredients are stated as explicit theorem fields in MainProofObligations, SliceProofObligations, HypergeometricProofObligations, ThreePointProofObligations, and GammaProofObligations.
