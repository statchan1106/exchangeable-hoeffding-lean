A sharper Hoeffding bound for weighted sums of exchangeable random variables

Seongchan Lee · formalization lead
Joint paper with Ilmun Kim · advisor and coauthor

Led by Seongchan Lee, this project connects the joint paper's statistical argument to Lean declarations. Every stage is labeled as either a closed proof or a conditional reduction, so the machine-checked boundary is visible at a glance.

What Lean checks now

No source-layout knowledge is needed: declarations are grouped by mathematical role.

closed proof

centeredWeightedSum_eq_centered_coordinates

Proves that the projected dot product is exactly \(\sum_i w_i(X_i-\overline X_N)\), using checked zero-padding and finite-sum identities.

closed proof

gamma_asymptotic_expansion

Identifies the maximizing finite-population term, proves the parity formula, and derives ΓN = 1 + 3/(2N) + O(N-2).

closed proof

hypergeometric_mgf_bound

Proves the sharpened sampling-without-replacement MGF by a finite first-draw recursion, induction, and complement symmetry.

closed proof

slice_mgf_bound_of_atMostTwoValues

Converts every zero-sum two-level vector into a centered hypergeometric count and proves the exact ΓN/8 bound.

conditional reduction

exchangeable_mgf_bound

Reduces the centered theorem to the zero-sum MGF field of MainAnalyticInputs. The reduction is checked; the input structure is not yet constructed in the repository.

Proof architecture

Follow declarations and roles, not source paths

  1. 01

    Center the statistic

    zeroPad → projOnePerp → centeredWeightedSum

    closed
  2. 02

    Use permutation invariance

    Exchangeable → exchangeability_supports_symmetrization

    closed assumption bridge
  3. 03

    Reduce to Hamming slices

    Exchangeable → permutation average → convex cube vertex → sliceAverage

    permutation/vertex assembly remains
  4. 04

    Identify a two-level extremizer

    threeObjective → ThreePointAnalyticInputs → AtMostTwoValues

    remaining analytic extremizer
  5. 05

    Bound the sampling MGF

    samplingMgf_step → hypergeometric_mgf_bound → hypergeomMgf_symm

    closed
  6. 06

    Assemble the probability bound

    SliceAnalyticInputs.slice_mgf_bound → exchangeable_mgf_bound → exchangeable_tail_bound_from_mgf

    slice consequence and Chernoff closed; outer assembly remains
