A Sharper Hoeffding Bound for Weighted Sums of Exchangeable Random Variables

Seongchan Lee and Ilmun Kim

A Lean 4 project for organizing and partially formalizing the proof of a sharper Hoeffding-type moment generating function bound for centered weighted sums of bounded exchangeable random variables. The current Lean development isolates the remaining analytic ingredients in an explicit no-global-axiom interface and proves the main theorem conditionally from that interface.

Paper draft in preparation; not yet on arXiv
